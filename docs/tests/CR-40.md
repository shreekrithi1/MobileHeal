**Priority:** High  
**Objective:** Verify new mandatory City field works and successful save shows success banner and stays on Profile.  
**Preconditions:** User is on Profile screen with editable fields.  
**Test data:** Name = `Alex Smith`, Email = `alex@example.com`, Date of Birth = `1990-05-20`, City = `Seattle`

| # | Step | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🤖 Enter valid name, email, DOB, and City. | Fields accept input. |
