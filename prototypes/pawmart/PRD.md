# PawMart

> Everything your pet needs, in one tap

## Idea
I want build a pet store app

## Problem
Pet owners struggle to quickly find the right products for their pets’ specific needs, compare options, and reorder essentials on time.

## Personas
- **Busy Pet Parent** — Quickly find and reorder trusted essentials for their dog with minimal effort.
- **First-Time Cat Owner** — Discover vetted products suitable for a new kitten and purchase a starter kit.

## MVP features
- Product catalog with search and filters (Must)
- Pet profiles to personalize recommendations (Must)
- Cart and checkout with guest flow (Must)
- Order history and one-tap reorder (Should)
- Basic promotions and coupon codes (Should)
- Product reviews and ratings (Could)
- Push notifications for low-stock/reorder reminders (Could)

## User stories
- As **Busy Pet Parent**, I want to find grain-free dog food quickly, so I can buy the right product without browsing endlessly.
  - [ ] Can search by keyword and filter by diet type and brand
  - [ ] Sees price, size variants, and stock status
  - [ ] Can add a selected variant to cart from product detail
- As **First-Time Cat Owner**, I want to create a pet profile, so I receive tailored product recommendations.
  - [ ] Can add pet name, species, breed, age, and dietary notes
  - [ ] Sees a personalized recommendations section
  - [ ] Can update pet details later
- As **Guest User**, I want to checkout without creating an account, so I can complete a purchase fast.
  - [ ] Can enter shipping, email, and payment details at checkout
  - [ ] Sees order summary with taxes and shipping before paying
  - [ ] Receives order confirmation with order number
- As **Returning Customer**, I want to reorder a previous purchase, so I can restock quickly.
  - [ ] Can view past orders with items and totals
  - [ ] Can add all or selected items to cart
  - [ ] Sees updated prices and stock before confirming

## Success metrics
- Conversion rate from product view to purchase
- Repeat purchase rate within 30 days
- Average time to checkout completion
